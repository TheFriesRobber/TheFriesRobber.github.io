A New Beginning

02/09/2019 Views
Thoughts

It has been years since I last wrote anything. A while ago, I tried to look back on what I had done and learned over those years, only to discover that I could reconstruct little more than scattered memories from my Chrome bookmarks. That was embarrassing. I had never developed the habit of organizing and recording what I learned. It was not that I had stopped learning, but that I was constantly taking things in without producing anything. I had also become very good at making excuses for myself.

Fortunately, it is never too late. Starting with this post, I want to share technical notes and personal reflections again. When I look back a few years from now, I hope I will have more than a collection of bookmarks to show for the time.
